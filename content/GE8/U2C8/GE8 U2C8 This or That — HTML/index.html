This or That?

A fast-and-fun preference game 🍦 ⚽ 🐱

“I prefer ___ to ___.”

Tap a picture to reveal its word · tap the dark bar for the sentence frame ·
← → arrows / space to change slides · press R to reveal everything

How to play 🎉

1Show a pair — the words are hidden! Ask: “This or that? … GO!”
2Students walk to the left side (pink) or the right side (teal).
3Tap a picture to reveal the word. Each side says the full sentence: “I prefer milkshakes to smoothies!”
4Tap the dark bar for the frame “I prefer ___ to ___.” — tap again to fill it in.
5Repeat rapidly with new pairs, faster each round. Loudest, clearest side wins! 🏆

Great job! 🌟

Now ask a friend — three new ideas:

“Do you prefer fish or meat?”I prefer fish to meat.
“Do you prefer English or Chinese?”I prefer English to Chinese.
“Do you prefer summer or winter?”I prefer summer to winter.
