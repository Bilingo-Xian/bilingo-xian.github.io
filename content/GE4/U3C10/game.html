How Many Animals Do You Have?
  1. 👫 Invite two students — one Asks, one Answers. (The class counts along!)
  2. 👀 Look at the animal waiting by the truck. The Asker asks: "How many … do you have?"
  3. ❓ Press Reveal Question — the animals hop into the truck! Count them aloud, together.
  4. 🔢 Press Reveal Answer — the truck brakes, the zoo gate rolls in, and the animals hop out! Everyone says the answer sentence.
  5. 🔁 Swap roles, press Next Delivery — morning, noon, evening or night, the zoo always needs animals!
