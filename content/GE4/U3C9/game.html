How Many Animals Can You See?
  1. 👫 Invite two students — one Asks, one Answers. (The class counts along!)
  2. 👀 Look at the animal. The Asker asks: "How many … can you see?"
  3. ❓ Press Reveal Question — the animals multiply! Count them aloud, together.
  4. 🔢 Press Reveal Answer — everyone says the answer sentence.
  5. 🔁 Swap roles, press Next Animal!
🎯 Target structures
❓ How many frogs can you see?
✅ I can see twelve frogs.
