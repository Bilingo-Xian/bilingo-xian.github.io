✏️ Key Structures
  • invite sb. to do sth.She invited me to come to her party. (邀请某人做某事)
  • have / has something to doI can't play — I have something to do. (有事情要做)
  • be sure to do sth.Be sure to knock first! (一定要做某事)
  • someone / anyone / everything / nothingSomeone is at the door! (复合不定代词)
  • drop sth. on sp.The parrot dropped food on my head! (把某物掉在某处)
  • look forI'm looking for my money. (寻找)
  • both of …Both of us love lemonade. (两者都……)
  • happen to sb. / sth.What happened to your voice? (某人/某物遭遇到……)
  • hear sb. / sth. doing sth.I heard someone crying. (听到……正在……)
  • as + adj. / adv. + as sb. canRun as fast as you can! (尽可能……)
