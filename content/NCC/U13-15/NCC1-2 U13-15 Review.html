✏️ Key Structures
  • on the left / on the rightThe cooker is on the left. The refrigerator is on the right.
  • in the middle of…The table is in the middle of the room.
  • There is / There are (proximity rule 就近原则)There is a cup and two bowls on the table. (cup is nearest → is)
  • a / an / the (articles)There is an electric cooker. The cup is clean.
  • some (肯定句) / any (否定句 & 疑问句)There are some cups. / Are there any cups?
  • Where is / are …?Where is the refrigerator? — It's on the right.
  • Imperatives (祈使句)Open the window! Shut the door! Sweep the floor!
  • must + verb (情态动词)You must make the bed. You must sweep the floor.
  • put sth. in sp.Put your clothes in the wardrobe.
  • put on / take off · turn on / turn offPut on your coat! / Take off your shoes! / Turn on the light!
