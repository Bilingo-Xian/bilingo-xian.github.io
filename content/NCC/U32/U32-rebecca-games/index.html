🧑‍🏫 Teacher cheat-sheet (offline extensions)
  • Before “Feed Rebecca”: ask Joey what HE eats when he's ill — “When I have a cold, I eat…” — then play.
  • Keep It Right → TPR: after each sentence, act it out (wrap a blanket, close the door, shush the baby). Then give silly commands: “Keep the teacher quiet!” 😄
  • Doctor, Doctor → reverse charades: after the quiz, Joey acts an illness, you guess: “You look terrible! Do you have an earache?”
  • Finish the Dialogue → role play: swap roles, then change the illness (measles! mumps!) and re-perform the call.
  • Sensible or Silly → make your own: Joey invents 2 silly sentences for YOU to judge. Kids love catching the teacher out.
  • Exit ticket: “Chocolate always makes me feel better” — agree or disagree? What makes you feel better? (…because…)
