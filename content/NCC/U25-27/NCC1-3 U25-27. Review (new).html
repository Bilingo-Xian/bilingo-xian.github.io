✏️ Key Structures
  • to tell you the truth (说实话，实话实说) To tell you the truth, I don't like steak.
  • either (也 — 用于否定句) I don't like beef. She doesn't like it either.
  • What is / are … like? (……怎么样？) What is the weather like in Italy?
  • visit + place (访问某地) She visits France every year.
  • It is + adjective (weather) (天气……) It is warm in spring. It is sometimes windy.
  • Where … come from? (来自哪里？) Where does she come from? She comes from Australia.
  • Which … do/does … like best? (最喜欢哪（个）？) Which season do you like best?
  • in the + direction (+ of) (在……方) Italy is in the south of Europe.
📝 Grammar Highlights

🔹 Simple Present — Third Person (一般现在时：第三人称)

He/She/It + verb-s: She likes lamb. He tells the truth.

✗ She like beef. → ✓ She likes beef.

🔹 Alternative Questions (选择疑问句)

Use "or" to give a choice. Answer by CHOOSING — don't say "yes" or "no"!

Do you like beef or lamb? → I like lamb. (NOT "Yes, I do.")

🔹 Zero Article with Countries (零冠词)

Most countries have NO article: She lives in France. He's from Japan.
Exception: the U.S., the U.K.

✗ She lives in the France. → ✓ She lives in France.

🔹 "like" — as a verb and a preposition

Verb: I like tea. Which do you like best?
Preposition: What is the weather like? (= describe it!)

What is Brazil like? — It's warm and beautiful!
