✏️ Key Structures
  • want to be / do …I want to be a pilot. / She wants to fly to Tokyo. (想要做……)
  • give sb. my regardsPlease give your mother my regards. (代我问候某人)
  • fly to sp.He will fly to Madrid next week. (飞往某地)
  • the week / month / year after nextI'm going to Berlin the year after next. (下下周/下下个月/后年)
  • plenty of …We have plenty of time. Don't worry! (很多，大量)
  • Why don't …?Why don't you have a quick drink? (为什么不……呢？)
  • look forward to …I'm looking forward to the trip! (期待……)
  • had better + doYou'd better hurry — the train leaves soon! (最好做……)
