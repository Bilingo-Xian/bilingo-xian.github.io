✏️ Key Structures
  • What colour is/are …?What colour is your hat? — It's green. (……是什么颜色的？)
  • the same + noun (+ as …)Her hat is the same colour as mine. (和……相同的)
  • come + adverbCome upstairs! / Come here! (来……)
  • … tooHis coat is smart. Her hat is smart, too. (也)
  • Are these/those …?Are these your passports? — Yes, they are. (这些/那些是……吗？)
  • Are your …?Are your friends Danish? — No, they aren't. (你的……是……吗？)
  • Come and meet …Come and meet my friend! (来见见……)
  • What are their jobs?What are their jobs? — They are sales reps. (他们是做什么工作的？)
