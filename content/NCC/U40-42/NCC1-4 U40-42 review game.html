✏️ Structures
  • not … at allI don't like vegetables at all.
  • a lot of … (many things / much + uncountable)There are a lot of things on the shopping list.
  • many (countable) / much (uncountable)many apples / much money
  • need + to + verbI need to buy some fruit.
  • have / has got = have / hasI've got a lot of money. = I have a lot of money.
  • thanks for + verb-INGThanks for cooking dinner!
  • nearly + adjectiveDinner is nearly ready.
  • have … for breakfast / lunch / dinnerWe had roast duck for dinner.
  • have + activity (bath, haircut, holiday, party)She's having a bath. / Let's have a party!
  • Let's + verbLet's go to the beach!
  • Present Perfect: have / has + past participleShe has packed her suitcase.
  • just / already + Present PerfectI've just left. / I've already packed.
