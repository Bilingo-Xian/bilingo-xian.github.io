✏️ Key Structures
  • What about …?What about a cup of milk? (= Would you like …?)
  • run acrossThe cat is running across the street. (跑着穿过)
  • run afterThe dog is running after the cat. (追赶，追逐)
  • look at …Look at the river! (看……)
  • fly overAn aeroplane is flying over the bridge. (飞过)
  • … out of …She is walking out of the building. (从……出来)
  • another + nounThere is another boat on the river. (另一个……)
  • Present Continuous: be + V-ingHe is swimming. / They are walking along the bank.
