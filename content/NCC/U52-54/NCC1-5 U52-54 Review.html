✏️ Key Structures
  • at the top of …He is at the top of the class. (在……首位/顶部)
  • How about …?How about this suit? (……怎么样？)
  • too + adj (+ to do)The exam is too difficult. / It's too heavy to carry. (太……)
  • adj + enough (+ to do)She's clever enough to pass. / It's not sweet enough. (足够……)
  • be full of …The dictionary is full of mistakes! (充满……)
  • tell sb. to do sth.Tell him to look it up. (告诉某人做某事)
  • look upLook up the word in the dictionary. (查阅)
  • Would you like (to) …?Would you like to try this jacket? (你想要……吗？)
  • … as wellI'd like a jacket as well. (也，同样)
  • Could you (please) …?Could you please carry this? (请你……好吗？)
  • Comparatives & Superlatives (regular)cheaper / more expensive / the cheapest / the most important
