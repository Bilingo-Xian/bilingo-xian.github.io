✏️ Key Structures
  • Shall I / we … ?Shall I give you some advice? (我/我们……好吗？)
  • a few + countable nounI'd like a few ideas, please. (几个)
  • a little + uncountable nounAdd a little less sugar — it's healthier! (一点儿)
  • less than …This flavour has less sugar than that one. (少/小于……)
  • pay for sth. in instalmentsShe paid for the laptop in instalments. (分期付款买某物)
  • sth. cost(s) sb. sth.The latest model cost her a lot. (某物花费某人……)
  • afford to do sth.I can't afford to buy it — the price is too high! (负担得起做某事)
  • get offThe passengers got off the bus. (下车/火车/飞机等)
  • none of sb. / sth.None of the passengers had any change. (没有一个……)
  • sb. have/has got no sth. = sb. haven't/hasn't got any sth.He's got no money. = He hasn't got any money. (某人没有某物)
