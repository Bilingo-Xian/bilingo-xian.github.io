✏️ Key Structures
  • forget to do sth.Don't forget to bring your coat! (忘记做某事)
  • take sth. with sb.Take an umbrella with you — it might rain! (随身携带某物)
  • do a (good / great) jobYou did a great job with the cake! (做得……)
  • attributive clause: who / which / thatThe man who wore the old coat is famous. (定语从句)
  • omitting that / which (object)The cake (that) we made was a big surprise. (关系代词的省略)
  • talk aboutEveryone talked about his big beard. (谈论起,说起)
  • offer sb. sth. = offer sth. to sb.He offered me a cup of tea. (提供给某人某物)
  • this / that + adjectiveWhy is your bag THAT heavy? (这么 / 那么……)
  • share (sth.) with sb.Share the cake with your friends! (和某人分享某物)
  • speak ofSpeaking of cake — I'm hungry! (说到,提起)
  • That means …No eggs? That means we can't make a cake! (那意味着……)
  • must / have to / need toYou must water the plants every day. (必须 / 不得不 / 需要)
