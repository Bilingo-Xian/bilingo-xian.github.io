✏️ Key Structures
  • hurry upHurry up! We're late for school! (快点！)
  • over thereThe teapot is over there. (在那边)
  • need to do (sth.)I need to find the kettle. (需要做某事)
  • ask sb. for sth.He asked his boss for a lift. (向某人要某物)
  • next door (to …)The bank is next door to the shop. (在……隔壁)
  • a minute / wait a minuteWait a minute! I can't find my keys! (等一会儿)
  • love sb. / sth.She loves her cat very much. (喜爱……)
  • like to do (sth.)I like to play football after school. (喜欢做某事)
  • can / can't + verb (情态动词 can)I can type. She can't read his handwriting. Can you help? (能/不能/能……吗？)
  • but (表示转折)I can make tea, but I can't make coffee. (但是)
