✏️ Key Structures
  • paint sth. + colourI'm going to paint it pink. (把……漆成……颜色)
  • sth. is/are for sb.The bookcase is for me. (某物是给某人的)
  • be going to + verbShe's going to paint the bookcase. (打算做某事)
  • in front of …The vase is in front of the window. (在……前面——外部)
  • in the front of …The teacher is in the front of the classroom. (在……前面——内部)
  • be careful / Don't …!Be careful! Don't drop the vase! (当心！不要……！)
  • send/show/take sb. sth. (双宾语)Send her a birthday card. / Show me the flowers.
  • a pound/piece/loaf/bar of …A loaf of bread. A bar of chocolate. (量词)
  • Is there any …? / There is some …Is there any sugar? — Yes, there's some. (there be 疑问句)
