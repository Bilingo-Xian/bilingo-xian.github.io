✏️ Key Structures
  • take sb. to sp.My dad takes me to school. (带某人去某地)
  • arrive in / atWe arrive at school at 8. / We arrive in Italy. (到达)
  • at noonWe eat lunch at noon. (在中午)
  • at the momentAt the moment, I'm doing my homework. (现在、此刻)
  • by + transportI go to school by bus. / I walk — I go on foot! (乘坐)
  • Do you want … or …?Do you want the big one or the small one? (你想要……还是……？)
  • What else …?What else do you want? (还要什么？)
